## Scenario multiplication (a general dimension, not engagement-specific)

When job-brief.md lists value scenarios, these sections repeat per scenario, with scenario-prefixed headers (215: "AS IS" / "AS COMPLETE/STABILIZED" / "MARKET RENT"; Elmore: As Is / As Renovated):
- Description of Improvements conclusions (condition per scenario)
- Each developed approach
- Correlation reconciles all scenarios; LIHTC adds Value of the Financing Incentives

Scenario sources: LIHTC (LURA restricted vs market rents + as-complete), renovation (Capital Needs Assessment), proposed construction (plans/budgets).

## Property-type variants

| Property type | Market Overview variant | Extra sections/inputs |
|---|---|---|
| Retail | Competing Retail Centers + Retail MarketPlace Profile | parking counts |
| Office | CoStar office submarket | tenant/suite mix |
| Industrial | industrial template | dock/clear-height emphasis in Improvements |
| Multi-family | renter-base demographics + multifamily submarket, unit-mix emphasis | rent roll, unit mix grid; LIHTC adds LURA/AMI docs |
| Special purpose (church) | often omitted | per-structure valuation, deferred-maintenance letter |

## Two-structure jobs
When distinct structures are valued separately (St. John Vianney church + rectory): Improvements and Sales Comparison repeat per structure, comps split into per-structure sets (`Comps/`, `Comps - Houses/`), one Correlation.

## Effective date rules
- Tax appeal: January 1 of assessment year (never the inspection date)
- Lending: inspection date, plus future dates for As Complete/Stabilized scenarios
- ROW: date specified by acquiring agency process
