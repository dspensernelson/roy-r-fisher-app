# Spenser's walk of the app, 4 September 2026

**This file does not delete itself.** It is not a plan. It is the record of
what Spenser asked for, in his own words, and what happened to each item.

**Why it exists.** He walked every screen click by click on 2026-09-04 and
marked each one Fine or Not fine. That list was never written down. The work
that followed has four commits and no document, so every session after it read
the commit messages and concluded it was a colour pass. On 2026-09-14 he ran
the result on Windows and asked what had happened to everything he asked for.
Recovering this cost an evening. It is written here so nobody pays that twice.

His list has no click 4 and has two click 5s. That is how he sent it.

**Two facts that change how to read this.** He sent the list at 6:18pm on
2026-09-04. Two of the four commits, `d4ee646` and `70a6fdc`, were written
before that and answer an earlier conversation about colour. Only `b8e6823`
answers this walk. `0732f3c` changes one line in VERSION.

---

## Built as he asked

**A loading screen.** Click 1. *"Can we make a loading screen?"* Built in
`b8e6823`. Reworked again on 2026-09-14 after it failed on Windows.

**A way back to the jobs list.** Click 10. *"there should be a 'Back to Jobs'
at the very top. We need to make it obvious these are fucking not computer
people."* Built in `b8e6823`.

**Settings, in two columns.** Click 11. *"We have boxes that are left, and
there's too much empty space on the right... There are 4: 1, 2, 3, 4. I think
this needs to be reconfigured... Make this feel a little more settingsy. Things
should be hidden more too."* **This one was recorded wrong and the record was
the fault.** The word red does not appear anywhere in what he said. He was
counting panels he wanted rearranged. It went into `docs/ROADMAP.md` as
*"Settings carried five red buttons and no way to tell which one mattered"* and
was closed by the colour rule; on 2026-09-14 the cards were given a gap. The
layout itself did not change until he asked again on 2026-09-15: *"why does the
setting screen still look like 5 panesl down instead of 1 | 2 / 3 | 4 / 5 |
6"*. Built that night. Two columns, the key card first, and the two paragraphs
of reassurance folded behind links. Written up in `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

**A rule for red and blue.** Click 8. *"it feels like we're doing a lot of red
as our action button, as opposed to blue... We do need a theory behind when red
and when blue"* Built in `b8e6823`. Eighteen red buttons became three. The rule
is in `docs/ROADMAP.md` under the colour section dated 2026-09-08.

   The re-walk never happened. 0.6.10 went to the virtual machine instead.

   screen, Settings in two columns, New job as two centred boxes, and Manage
   active jobs. The photo screen was rebuilt on 2026-09-15 and Settings went to
   two columns the same night. New job and Manage active jobs have not started.
4. **The app guessing the file name.** He said yes. Not built.
5. **The red close button beside Settings.** He was told "That's in a new slice.
   It's coming." Parked as already specified. Not built.
6. **A log line at every step of starting up**, promised after the Windows
   failure of the week before. `run_app.py` was reordered and no logging added.

---

**The count.** About twenty changes across eleven bad clicks. Four built as
asked. Two half answered. The rest untouched.

**The lesson, and it is the reason for this file.** A decision he states out
loud lives only in the session that heard it. Write it down the same day or it
is gone, and the next session will read the commits and believe those are the
whole story.
